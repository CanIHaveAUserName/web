About

Who I am.

I'm a sysadmin, webmaster, and software builder who likes understanding how the whole stack fits together, from a web page and API down to a Linux service, firewall rule, VM, or physical machine.

A lot of my work lives around game infrastructure, networking, virtualization, automation, VR, and self-hosted services. I run servers, build tools, troubleshoot hardware, and experiment with systems until I understand why they work.

I'm especially interested in practical infrastructure: keeping services online, making networks behave, squeezing performance out of hardware, and building things that are useful outside of a tutorial.
